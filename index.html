The Haney fitting

A boot that fits right makes a 12-hour shift shorter.

A safety toe that's too short or too narrow is miserable by lunch. We measure both feet, ask about your job, and have you walk in the boot before you buy.

Come in for a fitting

  1. Measure

    Length, width and arch on both feet. Most people are wearing the wrong size.

  2. Match the job

    Your site requirements, surfaces, hours on your feet and weather.

  3. Try & walk

    Compare toe caps and lasts across brands until one feels right.

  4. Finish it off

    Insoles, work socks and laces to get the most out of your boots.
